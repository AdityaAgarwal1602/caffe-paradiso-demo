Why “Paradiso”?

After Dante came Paradiso, the final canto, where love moves the sun and the other stars. Here it moves espresso. For the dreamers who dare to believe, and the coffee lovers who notice the details.

Est. 02.14.25. From the team behind Dante, Greenwich Village.

The ritual.

Order at the onyx bar

Pull up to the green stone counter. Choose a shakerato, a cream top, or just let the barista pick.

Cross Elizabeth Street

Seats inside are few and the garden is right across the street, so take your cup with you.

Sit with the flowers

Find a bench in Elizabeth Street Garden, take a sip and let your mind wander.
